#### Role And Responsiblities
* Developed custom tools to generate pathfinding graphs from tile maps 
* Engineered a noise-based distraction and detection system, inspired by Mark of the Ninja, integrated with AI and level design. 
* Programmed physics-based projectile simulation as a part of a throwing preview mechanic. 
* Created a drag-and-drop, grid-based inventory system for item management.
